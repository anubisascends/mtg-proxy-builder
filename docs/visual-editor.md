# Visual Page Editor

## Multi-Page Canvas

All pages are displayed vertically in a scrollable, zoomable canvas. The print order matches what you see on screen.

## Selection

- **Click** a card to select it (blue highlight)
- **Ctrl+Click** to add/remove individual cards from selection
- **Shift+Click** to select a range from the last clicked card (works across pages)
- **Ctrl+A** to select all, **Ctrl+D** to deselect, **Ctrl+Shift+I** to invert
- **Escape** or click empty space to deselect all
- **Arrow keys** to navigate between cards; **Shift+Arrow** for range selection

## Drag and Drop

Drag a card to reorder it to a new position on any page.

## Zoom & Pan

- **Ctrl+Mouse Wheel** to zoom in/out (15%-300%)
- **+/-** buttons, **Fit** to width, **1:1** reset in the bottom-right corner
- **Middle-click + drag** to pan freely
- **Shift+Mouse Wheel** for horizontal scrolling

## Right-Click Context Menu

- **Duplicate / Delete** — single card or all selected
- **Flip** — preview back artwork on the canvas
- **Match Back Art** — apply the project's most common back art to selected cards
- **Select Front Art** — change front artwork for selected cards
- **Select Card Back** — change back artwork for selected cards
- **Create Token Card** — for dual-faced cards: creates a new card with the front art and the project's common back

## Double-Click

Double-click a card to open the art selector dialog. If the card is flipped (showing back), it opens in back art mode.

## Overlay Text

Cards can have text overlays (e.g. "TOKEN") rendered on the front face in both the preview and exported PDF. Editable per card in the Card Details sidebar section.
